DESIGN & IMPLEMENTATION OF CLOSED-LOOP PID ULTRASONIC SYSTEM

1. Plant Model & Kinematic Linkage Specifications:

• Plant transfer function is an open-loop double integrator $G(s) = \frac{1.747}{s^2}$, requiring active derivative damping to achieve BIBO stability.

• Linkage ratio $K_{\text{linkage}} = \frac{r_{\text{arm}}}{L_{\text{linkage}}} = \frac{2.0\text{ cm}}{14.0\text{ cm}} \approx 0.1429^\circ\text{ beam / }^\circ\text{ servo}$.

2. Top Viva Questions & Answers:

Q: Why is derivative computed on measurement rather than error?
A: To eliminate "derivative kick"—an impulse spike in control effort that occurs when the setpoint undergoes an instantaneous step change.

Q: How does the anti-windup clamping work?
A: The integral accumulator is bounded between $\pm 15.0$ units. This prevents integrator saturation when an obstacle is held or out of range, eliminating massive overshoot upon target recovery.

Q: Why is an EMA pre-filter used on the ultrasonic distance?
A: HC-SR04 ultrasonic pulses exhibit high-frequency quantization jitter. A discrete EMA filter ($y_k = 0.3 d_k + 0.7 y_{k-1}$) suppresses spurious acoustic reflections with reduced phase lag.
